# Building and Evaluating Reliable AI Systems

I am a technology professional and AI researcher exploring agentic AI, multimodal systems, evaluation, and simulation-driven testing.

My work focuses on understanding how AI systems behave, identifying their failure modes, and developing methods to evaluate and improve their reliability.

## Research Interests

- **Agentic AI:** Multi-agent systems, agent workflows, and reliable tool use.
- **AI Evaluation:** Benchmarking, failure analysis, and reasoning reliability.
- **Multimodal AI:** Robustness, model behavior, and controlled experiments.
- **AI Safety and Interpretability:** Understanding capabilities, limitations, and failure modes.
- **Simulation and Testing:** Reproducible experiments, scenario generation, and evaluation infrastructure.

## Background

I hold a Master's degree in AI and Machine Learning from IIT Kanpur, an MBA from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, and a background in Computer Science.

My 15+ years of experience span software engineering, technology innovation, entrepreneurship, venture capital, and startup ecosystem development, including roles at Philips, NXP, ST-Ericsson, and IvyCap Ventures/IvyCamp.

## Current Focus

I am exploring research-driven approaches to evaluating AI systems through controlled experiments, reproducible benchmarks, and simulation-based testing.

I am particularly interested in translating these methods into practical tools and infrastructure for building more reliable AI applications.
